Upcoming Initiatives & Open Calls

Here's what’s coming up:

The Spotlight Series

This initiative aims to recognize and reward the exceptional efforts of IEEE Student Chapters, WIE Affinity Groups, and SIGHT Groups for their impactful activities during a defined period. The IEEE SAC Student newsletter is on the way, and we want to highlight the best student-led initiatives across Tunisia.

Did your OU recently organize a workshop, event, technical visit, or impactful activity? Submit it now and inspire others!

How does it work?
- Each unit can apply for the category that best reflects their strengths and achievements.
- The categories are flexible and can be chosen based on your unit’s focus, interests, past activities, or your positioning.
- You’ll submit a brief form and report highlighting your unit’s activities and outcomes within the eligible period.

Why should you apply?
- Boost your unit’s visibility in the section
- Inspire other units to take action and aim higher
- Earn a digital certificate and social media spotlight

Submissions will be open soon for April and May! Let’s showcase the amazing work you’ve done and continue building a stronger student ecosystem together.
