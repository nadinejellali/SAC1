SAC Initiatives

IEEE TSLW 2.0: 20 July in Verdi Hotel

The second edition of the IEEE Tunisian Student Leadership Workshop was held on July 20, 2025, at the Verdi Tunis Beach Resort in Gammarth, uniting IEEE student leaders, counselors, volunteers, and professionals for a day focused on leadership development and collaboration.

Organized by the IEEE Tunisia Section Student Activities Committee, the event featured speakers from IEEE Tunisia Section and IEEE Region 8, along with interactive workshops, panel discussions, and networking sessions. Participants explored IEEE resources, shared best practices, and strengthened connections among student branches and counselors.

IEEE Student Branch Leaders Meeting in MENA SYP Congress

During the Region 8 MENA SYP, the IEEE SAC Tunisia session hosted a Student Branch Leaders Meeting, where the energy in the room was truly vibrant. The Spotlight Series

This initiative aims to recognize the exceptional efforts of IEEE Tunisia section Student: Branches, Chapters, WIE Affinity Groups, and SIGHT Groups for their impactful activities during a defined period. The IEEE Tunisia SAC Student newsletter is on the way, and we want to highlight the student led initiatives across Tunisia.

Did your OU recently organize a workshop, event, technical visit, or impactful activity?
Submit it now and inspire others!

How does it work?

  • Each unit can apply for the category that best reflects their strengths and achievements.
  • The categories are flexible and can be chosen based on your unit’s focus, interests, past activities, or your positioning.
  • You will submit a brief form and report highlighting your unit’s activities and outcomes within the eligible period.

Why should you apply?

  • Boost your unit’s visibility in the section
  • Inspire other units to take action and aim higher
  • Earn a digital certificate and social media spotlight
